# Case studies

Real analyses done end to end in Data Boss. Each folder has the write-up, the report exported from the app, the session as a Jupyter notebook and the charts.

| | Question | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| [01](01-baghdad-gyms) | **What makes a top-rated gym in Baghdad?** | Data-quality checks, cleaning, statistical tests, and a trust-adjusted rating that fixes "5 stars from 2 reviews" |
| [02](02-bike-sharing) | **Two years of Washington bike sharing** | Trends and year-over-year growth, weekday patterns of two kinds of rider, weather effects, and a backtested forecast that admits what two years can't tell |
| [03](03-bank-marketing) | **Who will subscribe? An honest model of a bank's phone campaign** | Three models against a baseline on imbalanced data, why accuracy misleads, and the call-duration leakage trap |
